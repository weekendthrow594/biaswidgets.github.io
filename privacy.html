Bias Widgets — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Bias Widgets mobile application for iOS and Android (the "App"), developed by Li ("we", "us", or the "Service Provider"). The App is offered as a freemium service and is provided "as is".

This document explains what information is processed when you use the App, why it is processed, who may receive it, how long it may be retained, and the choices available to you. By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Your widget content stays local. Names, dates, photos, quotes, and other content you add to a widget are stored on your device. We do not upload that content to our servers, Firebase, or AdMob, and we do not use it to personalize advertising.

1. Information We Process

1.1 Technical and Advertising Data

When the App connects to remote services or displays advertising, the App and the third-party services listed in Section 4 may automatically collect or process:

  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system and version, app version, language, and general configuration
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a general region; the App does not request or collect precise GPS location
  • User and advertising interactions, such as app launches, taps, ad impressions, ad clicks, and video views
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as crash data, app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage
  • Device, app, or advertising identifiers, such as Android advertising ID, Android app set ID, Apple IDFA where permission is granted, Apple IDFV, SDK-generated identifiers, and SKAdNetwork attribution data
  • Advertising and consent information, such as ads shown, consent status, privacy choices, and signals needed to select, measure, limit, and protect advertising

The exact data available depends on your device, platform, region, consent choices, advertising settings, and the advertising mode used.

1.2 Content You Provide

The App lets you create and personalize widgets using information such as names, dates, photos, quotes, subtitles, and emoji selections. This content is stored in app-private storage on your device and is shared only with the App's widget components as needed to display your widgets:

  • On iOS, the App uses Apple Core Data and an Apple App Group container to make content available to its widget extension.
  • On Android, the App uses app-private files, DataStore, and Glance widget state to make content available to its widgets.

We do not upload this content to our servers or use it for advertising. Depending on your operating-system backup and device-transfer settings, Apple or Google may include app data in a device backup or transfer under their own privacy policies.

1.3 Remote Library Content

The App uses Firebase Firestore to download remote content and configuration, including quote, countdown, and emoji library resources. Your locally created widget content is not uploaded to Firestore. When the App requests remote content, Google may process technical connection data needed to provide and secure the service.

1.4 Purchase Information

If purchases or subscriptions are offered on your platform, the transaction is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, as applicable. We may receive a transaction identifier, receipt or purchase token, product information, and subscription or entitlement status so that the App can provide purchased features. We do not receive your payment card details, Apple ID password, or Google Account password.

2. How We Use Information

We and our service providers may use the information described above:

  • To provide, maintain, and synchronize the App and its widgets on your device
  • To retrieve remote library content and configuration
  • To display personalized, non-personalized, limited, or contextual advertising as permitted by your choices and applicable law
  • To measure advertising, cap ad frequency, analyze performance, and prevent invalid traffic, fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • To diagnose problems, improve reliability, and develop App functionality
  • To verify purchases, subscriptions, and entitlements where those features are offered
  • To comply with applicable law and enforce legal rights

3. Legal Bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on one or more of the following:

  • Your consent, including consent requested for personalized advertising, cookies or similar local storage, and tracking where required
  • Performance of a contract, when processing is needed to provide features or purchases you request
  • Our legitimate interests, such as maintaining the App, preventing fraud, securing services, measuring performance, and providing non-personalized advertising where permitted
  • Compliance with legal obligations

You may withdraw consent through the controls described in Section 5. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal.

5. Advertising and Privacy Choices

The App may show personalized, non-personalized, limited, or contextual ads. Personalized ads may use previously collected information to infer interests. Non-personalized ads are not based on past behavior, although identifiers or local storage may still be used for purposes such as frequency capping, aggregated reporting, security, and fraud prevention. Limited ads disable personalization and features that require local identifiers.

Consent and Privacy Options

Where required, the App uses Google's UMP consent message before requesting ads. If a privacy-options entry is available in the App's Help page, you can use it to review or change eligible choices. You may also manage advertising preferences through your device settings and Google My Ad Center.

iOS App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, the App requests permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before accessing the IDFA or tracking you across apps and websites owned by other companies when that permission is required. You can change this choice in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Android Advertising Controls

On Android, you can reset or delete the Android advertising ID and manage supported ad-privacy controls in your device settings. Available controls and menu names vary by device manufacturer and Android version.

If you decline or restrict personalized advertising, you may still see non-personalized, limited, or contextual ads.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your locally created widget content or disclose it to advertising providers. We may disclose the technical information described in Section 1.1:

  • To the service providers listed in Section 4 for the purposes described in this Policy
  • To comply with applicable law, legal process, or a valid government request
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of the Service Provider, users, or the public, or to investigate fraud, invalid traffic, abuse, or security incidents
  • In aggregated or de-identified form that cannot reasonably identify you

We do not sell personal information for money. Some privacy laws may define the disclosure of identifiers or advertising data for interest-based advertising as a "sale," "sharing," or "targeted advertising." Where required, you may opt out or change your choices through the privacy controls described in Section 5 or by contacting us.

7. Data Retention

Content you create in the App remains in app-private storage until you delete it or uninstall the App. Uninstalling generally removes local App data, subject to operating-system backup, restoration, and device-transfer settings.

We do not set the retention periods used by Google, Apple, or other independent service providers. Technical, advertising, consent, transaction, and diagnostic data is retained according to each provider's settings, legal obligations, and privacy policy. We retain information under our control only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy or as required by law.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a portable copy of personal data, withdraw consent, opt out of certain advertising disclosures, and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

You can edit or delete locally created content in the App, change eligible advertising choices as described in Section 5, or stop using the App and uninstall it. Because locally created content is not uploaded to us, we generally cannot access or delete that content on your behalf. To make a request regarding data that you believe we control, contact us using the address in Section 12.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Where a higher minimum age applies, including in parts of the European Economic Area, the applicable higher age governs.

If you believe a child under the applicable minimum age has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information we process. User-created content is stored in platform-provided app-private storage and shared with the App's own widget components through iOS App Groups or Android widget state, as applicable. Data transmitted to supported Google services is protected in transit using encryption such as Transport Layer Security (TLS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where required, provide additional notice. Your continued use of the App after an updated policy takes effect is subject to the revised policy.
