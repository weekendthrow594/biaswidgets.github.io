Bias Lingo Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 7, 2026
Document version: bias-lingo-2026-11-07-v1

Bias Lingo (the “App”) is developed and operated by Li (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains how information is handled when you use our iOS caption and translation service. Contact us at danwidget@hotmail.com about privacy or your data.

Local mode: speech recognition and translation run on your device. An account is not required.

Fast mode: after your separate permission, app audio is sent to ElevenLabs for speech recognition. Translation continues to run on your device.

Your history: the App stores caption and translation history on your device, not in our cloud account database. Account information, minutes, consent records and account-deletion administration are handled separately online.

1. Screen sharing, audio and captions

You choose when to start and stop the iOS screen-sharing session. The App uses audio made available by the system from other apps to generate captions. Screen frames received through the capture interface are discarded; the App does not upload screen video or save audio or video recordings to its history.

In Local mode, Apple’s on-device speech recognition processes audio. In both modes, Apple Translation processes translation on the device. Language-model downloads and other system operations may contact Apple under Apple’s terms. Using Local mode does not send your captured audio to ElevenLabs or our Firebase backend.

In Fast mode, after you sign in, give separate AI permission and start sharing, the App sends audio over an encrypted connection directly to ElevenLabs, Inc. using Scribe v2 Realtime. ElevenLabs receives audio, which may include voices, background sounds or personal information spoken in the content, and returns original-language text and timing information. Language and connection metadata are also processed. Our Firebase credential service supplies temporary access credentials; it does not relay or store this audio or the recognition results.

Fast mode is optional. You can refuse it and use Local mode, stop sharing, or withdraw permission in Settings → Third-Party AI Services. Turning it off stops audio sending on that device immediately; an account-level withdrawal waits for an internet connection if you are offline. Withdrawal does not undo processing already performed. Stop sharing on other devices as well if they are in use.

We do not use your audio or subtitle history to train our own models. This is not a promise of zero retention by ElevenLabs: its handling of request data is subject to its applicable service terms, data-processing arrangements and account settings. The current integration does not guarantee that ElevenLabs immediately deletes every request. See its Privacy Policy and data-retention explanation. Do not share content that you are not entitled to have processed, or sensitive content that you do not want transmitted in Fast mode.

2. Information stored on your device

Caption text, translations, history titles, language selections and available timing information are stored locally using Core Data. Preferences and pending operation identifiers are also stored locally. The App does not upload or synchronize this history through Firebase, CloudKit or an account-history service. Signing in on another device does not restore it from our servers.

History is associated with the installation, not separated into cloud accounts. Other people with access to the same unlocked installation may see it, including after a different account signs in. You can delete history in the App. Account deletion and signing out do not remotely erase it. Your own operating-system backup, device-transfer or export choices may create copies outside the App’s local storage; manage those copies using the relevant system or service.

3. Accounts, minutes and consent records

We use Google Firebase Authentication for email/password, Google and Apple sign-in. Account data can include your Firebase user identifier, email address (including an Apple relay address), name or profile details supplied by the sign-in provider, provider identifiers and sign-in/security metadata. Email/password credentials are handled by Firebase; we do not receive your Google or Apple password.

Firebase and Google Cloud host the backend for account-linked minutes, daily grants, reward claims, session permissions, duplicate-request protection and abuse prevention. Records include account identifiers or their hashes, dates, balances, charge and reward identifiers, session state, expiry times and request outcomes. They do not include subtitle text, translations, audio or history titles.

We also keep the version and document fingerprints of the service agreement and AI disclosure you accepted, whether you granted or withdrew permission, revision and event identifiers, recorded times, app version, interface language and where the choice was made. These records help apply your choices consistently and explain what you agreed to. A privacy-policy acknowledgement is separate from permission to send audio to ElevenLabs.

4. Security and diagnostic information

Firebase App Check with Apple App Attest helps check app authenticity. The infrastructure and service providers may process IP addresses, app/device identifiers, attestation and authentication tokens, device or software characteristics, request times and security signals. Our operational logs record error codes, latency, request outcomes and operation identifiers to diagnose problems and limit abuse.

Development or troubleshooting logs on your device may contain caption text. They are not automatically uploaded as history. If you choose to send logs, screenshots, recordings or support emails to us, we receive what you send; remove unrelated or sensitive information first. The current App does not integrate Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics, but advertising providers have their own measurement and diagnostic processing described below.

5. Optional rewarded advertisements

You may choose to watch a Google AdMob rewarded ad for additional Fast minutes. Google Mobile Ads and its User Messaging Platform handle advertising and applicable privacy choices. Ad-related processing may include IP address and approximate location, device/app identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic/performance data. This supports ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention; personalization depends on applicable choices and platform permissions. The App does not provide your audio or subtitle history to the ad SDK as advertising input.

We record the claim identifier, account, date, reward outcome and awarded minutes to prevent repeated rewards. An ad completion event does not itself prove that minutes have been credited; the backend confirms the result. Where available, use the advertising privacy-options control in Settings and your iOS privacy settings to manage advertising choices. The App does not bypass iOS tracking permissions. Local captions do not require watching ads.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and its Mobile Ads data disclosure.

6. Deletion requests and service email

You can request deletion in Settings → Account. We store the request identifier, account identifier, account email, language, status and relevant timestamps to process it and allow cancellation. Requests become eligible for manual handling after seven days; the current service aims to complete deletion within fourteen days of the request. You can cancel until processing begins. Signing in does not automatically cancel a request: you must expressly choose to cancel it.

While a request is pending, Fast mode and rewards are paused; Local mode remains available. An authorized administrator rechecks the request before removing the account and its associated cloud minutes and consent records. We send a completion notice to the account email where available. If you cannot access the App or need help with a data-rights request, contact us by email.

We use Resend to deliver deletion notices and internal pending-request summaries. It processes delivery addresses, message content and delivery metadata. Internal summaries include the account/request identifiers and dates needed for an administrator to act, not your subtitle history. See Resend’s Privacy Policy. These are service messages, not marketing subscriptions.

Deleting your App account does not delete your Apple or Google account, remove local history, or erase copies you supplied to others. You can also manage the App’s sign-in connection in the relevant provider’s account settings.

7. Purposes, sharing and international processing

We process information to provide the requested service, manage accounts and minutes, record privacy choices, deliver rewards and service notices, answer support requests, secure the service and meet applicable legal duties. Where a legal basis is required, this includes performance of our agreement, consent for optional processing where required, legitimate interests in secure and reliable operation where permitted, and legal obligations. You can withdraw consent for future optional processing without affecting processing lawfully performed before withdrawal.

Information is disclosed to the providers described above for those functions, to authorized administrators handling support or deletion, and where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights and security. We do not sell your audio or history or provide it for advertising. Advertising identifiers and activity are subject to the separate ad processing and choices described in section 5; this policy does not characterize all advertising processing as exempt from local “sale” or “sharing” rules.

Google Firebase/Cloud, ElevenLabs, Apple, Resend and their service providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Our current cloud functions and quota database are hosted in the United States. Data-protection rules may differ; applicable provider contractual safeguards and legally required transfer measures apply. Taiwan time used for quota resets does not mean that data is hosted in Taiwan. See Firebase’s privacy information and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

8. Retention and protection

  • Local history remains until you delete it or remove the App’s local data, subject to backups you control.
  • Cloud account, quota and consent records are retained while needed to operate the account, resolve requests and protect the service. Expiry of daily minutes does not automatically delete the associated records. Account deletion removes the associated account ledger and consent events.
  • Pending deletion requests remain until cancelled or processed. Cancellation removes the stored raw account identifier and email from the request, while minimal request/version metadata remains to reject delayed duplicate actions. After deletion, a minimal hashed-account completion receipt is scheduled for removal after thirty days to prevent stale access and repeated processing.
  • Queued or failed service emails remain while delivery or manual resolution is pending. After our mail service accepts a message, we remove its address and body from our delivery queue; the remaining status receipt is scheduled for removal after thirty days. Automated expiry is asynchronous. Provider logs and recipients’ email copies have their own retention.
  • Support correspondence and technical/security logs are retained only as needed for their purpose, troubleshooting and applicable legal duties. Some records may need longer retention for a specific legal requirement or dispute. Supplier-held audio and request data are subject to section 1, not a local-history deletion promise.

We use encrypted network connections, access controls and restricted server-side credentials. No service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device and account secure; do not send passwords or API secrets to support.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, a copy, deletion, restriction or objection to processing of your personal data, withdraw consent, and complain to a competent data-protection authority. Contact danwidget@hotmail.com. We may request proportionate information to verify account ownership, but will not ask for your password. We respond within applicable legal deadlines; the in-app waiting period does not limit mandatory rights. For history held only on your device, use the App’s history controls—we cannot retrieve it from a cloud backup we do not maintain.

10. Children and changes to this policy

The App is not directed to children under thirteen, or a higher minimum age required by local law for the relevant service. We do not knowingly seek personal data from children below that age. If you believe such data was provided, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Users who are not legally able to agree on their own need an authorized parent or guardian’s involvement.

We may update this policy as the service changes. We identify the version and date and provide appropriate notice. Material changes to optional AI recipients or purposes require renewed permission where necessary. A routine app update does not by itself create new permission. The version shown when you accept is retained as a record; later website edits do not change that record.
